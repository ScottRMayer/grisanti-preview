About the firm

A defense built
from the other side of the case file.

Most attorneys learn the law from the outside looking in. Joseph D. Grisanti spent years working it from the inside — first as a Special Agent with the Federal Bureau of Investigation, then as a Prosecutor, before opening his Brick, New Jersey practice.

That trajectory shapes everything about how this firm approaches a case. Police reports get read the way an investigator wrote them. Charges get evaluated the way a prosecutor would weigh them at trial. And the work begins with the same question the other side is asking: what does the file actually prove — and what doesn't it?

For three decades, that perspective has guided real estate closings, estate plans, personal injury claims, and the full range of municipal court defense across Ocean County and beyond. Every client works directly with the attorney. Every case gets the same preparation.

How we work

Straightforward,
start to finish.

No call centers. No intake forms standing between you and the attorney. Here's what working with the firm actually looks like.

— I.

The free consultation

You call the office and speak with the attorney directly. We listen to the facts, identify the legal questions in play, and tell you honestly whether you have a matter worth pursuing — and what it will cost if you do.

— II.

The review

If you engage the firm, we read every document. Police report. Contract. Lab result. Witness statement. We approach the file the way the other side did, and we look for what they missed.

— III.

The resolution

Whether that means a negotiated outcome, a closing table, an executed estate plan, or a courtroom — you get the same preparation either way. And you hear it from the attorney, not a message left with someone else.
